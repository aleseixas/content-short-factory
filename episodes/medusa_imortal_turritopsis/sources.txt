Matsumoto et al. (2019) — Transcriptome Characterization of Reverse Development in Turritopsis dohrnii

Used for physical damage/adverse conditions/aging as triggers, cyst stage, return to polyp, and transdifferentiation context.

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9835754/
Used for evidence that repeated life-cycle reversion has been experimentally observed.
